| ADR-035 | Model tank water as one well-mixed compartment per tank with explicit units (litres, mg/L dissolved oxygen, mg/L ammonia nitrogen, grams of food). | ADR-036 | World save v2 adds per-tank water, and world v1 migrates with default water. | ADR-037 | Life model v1 stores age, current length and a lagged condition per fish. | ADR-049 | 14 Sep 2026: normal breeding (world v5) splits hard pairing rules (roles, adult stage, condition, rest, one courtship per fish and per nursery, a shared tank, free nursery places, population limits) from soft courtship pauses (separation, condition, harsh water). | ADR-050 | 14 Sep 2026: the family view (FS-404) is a bounded ancestor graph computed on demand. | ADR-051 | 14 Sep 2026: kinship comes from one session cache (FS-405). | ADR-055 | 15 Sep 2026: naming model 2 (user request) names every new fish, whether bred, laid in a clutch, bought or delivered to the shop, from 1, 2 or 3 parts in equal shares: a noun from 555, with a prefix from 213 and/or a suffix from 207 (about 24.7 million names). | ADR-056 | 15 Sep 2026: naming model 3 (user request) never lets a name claim a trait the fish lacks. Every word belongs to a group. The neutral group (91 prefixes, 280 nouns, 142 suffixes) holds no color, pattern, size, shape, behavior, age, lineage or sex words. Every other word needs tags derived from the fish's sex and expressed adult phenotype. Body colors need one expressed variant: a blend is named for neither color. Accent colors need the accent to be drawn (patches, filled fins, marble, calico, rosettes, fin edges or flame). Patterns, eyes, scales and sheen follow the appearance model. Size, tail, shape, whiskers, eyes, appetite, turning, speed and the four inherited tendencies use thresholds near the founder tenth and ninetieth percentiles or beyond. About half the fish with a nameable trait get one trait part, at most one part per trait group. Names that could lie for reasons outside the genome, such as "Old", "Jr." or "Grandpa", are gone. A world v7 snapshot named under an older model replays under the current model and is compared without fish names, listing names or ledger text, then rebases onto model 3. The numbered and model 2 generators are removed | A name is a claim players read beside the portrait, so "Golden" on a jade fish or "Tiger" without stripes is wrong, and so is "Queen" on a male. Deriving claims from the same phenotype the renderer and inspector use keeps names honest without new state. Size and colors describe adult genetic potential, which the inspector already labels. Comparing older snapshots without names keeps every existing save loading without freezing past word lists, and names remain mutable text | Names that change as a fish grows or is recolored, trait words for lineage (for example "Jr." for a son of a same-named father), or localized vocabularies |
